ScamShield Jr is an offline scam message explainer for families. It helps a student slow down a suspicious message with a grandparent.

The examples are synthetic. The analyzer never sends anything away and never decides that a message is safe.

In this locked account example, the engine finds a countdown, a password request, a mismatched sender domain, and a different reply-to domain.

Each verdict points to exact evidence. The marks show the matched characters, the field, and the offsets that fired the rule.

The summary still names what the offline tool cannot check. It cannot open a live link or prove who controls the sender account.

Drill mode gives the student six practice messages. Three are scams and three are legitimate but suspicious, so judgment is practiced instead of memorized.

The safer outcome is a pause, a read aloud script, and a trusted phone call. One limitation remains. Families still verify the real message themselves.
