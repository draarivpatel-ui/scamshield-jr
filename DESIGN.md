# ScamShield Jr design system

## Visual world

ScamShield Jr uses an indexed family field notebook. The world is cool ledger paper, deep ink, ledger teal and evidence amber, with restrained alarm red only when a strong signal appears. The interface stays an Operate surface first. The notebook contributes palette, density, type rhythm and one signature move rather than controlling the layout.

The signature move is the evidence index. Exact matched spans carry small `E1` to `E8` markers that correspond to tactic cards, so a family can move from proof to explanation without hunting.

## Typography

Atkinson Hyperlegible is the primary face because the product is for family reading across ages and visual needs. It is self-hosted and used at 400 and 700. `ui-monospace` is reserved for evidence text, offsets and filenames because those are measurements and source data.

Display type is large, tight and plain. No eyebrow or kicker labels appear above headings, and no tagline sits under the title.

## Color

- Ink `#17243a` for the top bar, verdict panel and primary text.
- Paper `#f4efe3` and soft white `#fffdf7` for the work surface.
- Ledger teal `#0d6963` for primary actions, selected states and evidence markers.
- Evidence amber `#c98319` for moderate signals, highlights and focus.
- Alarm red `#ad3d39` only for strong signals.
- Muted blue gray for secondary text and rules.

Color carries state and evidence meaning. The interface does not use a binary green safe state.

## Composition

The first viewport is a three-column workbench at desktop width. The title and mode controls sit in a narrow notebook rail, message intake occupies the center, and the verdict panel stays visible on the right. Evidence and tactic verdicts form a two-column reading grid below. Drill mode compresses the title rail and moves the six drill choices into a persistent left column.

Cards use full borders and modest radii. Primary actions carry the only strong elevation. Background ruling is subtle notebook paper, not decoration separate from the world.

## Motion

One authored moment exists after analysis. A soft scan passes over the evidence notebook, then stops. Reduced motion disables it. State changes otherwise use short functional transitions.

## Accessibility

The interface uses semantic forms, labels, buttons, fieldsets and live regions. Focus rings are thick and amber. The mobile layout collapses to one column and is checked for horizontal overflow. No meaning is carried by color alone because tactic cards also name severity in text.
